Chapter 1

How a Voice Gets Out into the World

5

01

A Translator That Keeps My Way of Speaking

It polishes the sentences but keeps the verbal habits, the humor and the rhythm, so it sounds exactly like that person said it.

6

02

An Audiobook in My Own Voice

It clones your own voice, then reads the book you wrote back to you in it.

7

03

A News Channel with Its Own Anchors

Easy news planned and hosted by people with developmental disabilities themselves. AI handles the script, captions and editing.

8

04

Lyricist and Composer

A few hums and a few words become a finished song, released for everyone to stream.

9

05

My Life, as a Webtoon

What you said and the photos you took in a day become one webtoon episode every week.

10

06

Translating the Unspoken

It turns gestures, expressions, sounds and drawings into sentences. Someone who had no words gets sentences of their own for the first time.

Chapter 2

The World Adjusts to Them

12

07

Easy-World Glasses

Through smart glasses, signs, menus and contracts change into plain words and pictures.

13

08

A Coach in the Ear

During a conversation, an earpiece whispers, “That was a joke,” or “Saying thank you here would be nice.”

14

09

“My Mode” on Every Website

Banks, government portals and shopping sites rebuild themselves automatically to the level and speed that suit that person.

15

10

Easy Verdicts, Easy Manifestos

The moment a court ruling or an election document is published, a version made just for you appears.

16

11

Life Rehearsal VR

Live your first day at work, your first move, your first hospital visit ten times before the real one.

Chapter 3

The Other Way Around: They Teach the World and AI

18

12

AI’s “Understanding Panel”

AI companies around the world ask a panel of people with the disability, “Can our explanation really be understood?” If these people understand it, older adults, children and foreigners will too.

19

13

Teacher of Care Robots

A job teaching robots: “This is how I need to be helped to feel at ease.”

20

14

The Model for Kind AI

An AI personality that learned from conversations with them: never in a hurry, never tired of repeating, honest. In the end, the AI everyone uses becomes kinder.

21

15

A Way of Thinking Is an Asset

Their way of grasping the abstract through concrete experience becomes the world standard for a “good explanation.”

Expressing Thought Directly

The Category “Intellectual Disability” Disappears

Just as glasses stopped making nearsightedness a disability, once cognitive support is as natural as air, the distinction of “low intelligence” loses its meaning. What remains is only differences in personality, taste and experience.
