  <p class="eyebrow">About</p>
  <h1>We turn questions about learning<br>into work that lasts.</h1>
  <p>Nalja Archive publishes research evidence, educational perspectives, case-based reflection, and policy explanations developed by Nalja Books and Nalkku.</p>
</header>

<div class="prose page-shell reading-shell">
  <h2>Who is this archive for?</h2>
  <p>We write primarily for families of people with intellectual disabilities, special educators and practitioners, welfare organizations, researchers, and related groups. Terms are explained from the beginning, and each article presents its central answer early so that readers do not need specialist knowledge to enter the discussion.</p>

  <h2>Who writes the articles?</h2>
  <p>The default author is the <strong>Nalja Books and Nalkku Editorial Team</strong>. Each article shows its publication and revision dates and links to the primary sources used in its preparation.</p>

  <h2>What information is not repeated here?</h2>
  <p>Company information, product details, and purchasing guidance are maintained on the <a href="https://naljabooks.com">official Nalja Books website</a>.</p>
</div>
