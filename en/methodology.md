<header class="page-hero page-shell reading-shell">
  <p class="eyebrow">Editorial policy</p>
  <h1>We explain clearly<br>and leave a path for verification.</h1>
  <p>Nalja Archive applies the following principles so readers can distinguish claims from sources and general information from the publisher's own perspective.</p>
</header>

<div class="prose page-shell reading-shell numbered-sections">
  <section>
    <span>01</span>
    <h2>We prioritize primary sources.</h2>
    <p>Research findings and definitions are checked against academic papers, government and international-organization documents, and official materials from specialist institutions. We do not rely on summary articles alone and provide an accessible source URL and publication year whenever possible.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <span>02</span>
    <h2>We separate general information from Nalja's perspective.</h2>
    <p>Findings supported by research or public documents are not presented as identical to the educational perspective adopted by Nalkku. When we describe a product or activity, we state its scope and limitations and do not guarantee outcomes.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <span>03</span>
    <h2>We record revisions.</h2>
    <p>When a source, figure, product detail, or explanation changes, we update the article's revision date. If a correction changes the central conclusion, the article identifies what changed.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <span>04</span>
    <h2>We protect people represented in examples.</h2>
    <p>Identifiable examples require the consent of the person concerned or a legally authorized representative. We omit personal information that is not necessary and describe adolescents and adults with developmental disabilities as people who learn and make choices.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <span>05</span>
    <h2>We investigate reported errors.</h2>
    <p>If you find incorrect information or a broken source, please use the contact channel on the <a href="https://naljabooks.com">official Nalja Books website</a> and include the article title and the passage in question. We will recheck the primary source and make any necessary correction.</p>
  </section>
</div>
